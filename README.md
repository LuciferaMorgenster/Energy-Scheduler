A daily planner that schedules your day around your personal energy levels (your chronotype) rather than strict hours. You input your peak focus times and low-energy dips, and the app recommends the ideal moments to create, plan, exercise, or unwind.

**Energy Scheduler** is an intuitive, chronotype-based daily planning app for Android. Designed around biological energy rhythms rather than rigid clock hours, Energy Scheduler helps users map out tasks according to their peak focus times and low-energy dips for optimal focus, creativity, and rest.

---

## ✨ Features

- ⚡ **Chronotype Mapping:** Easily configure your daily energy peaks and low-energy dips.
- 🎯 **Smart Activity Recommendations:** Get tailored suggestions on when to create, plan, exercise, or unwind.
- 🎨 **Minimalist Design:** Clean, distraction-free UI designed to reduce planning fatigue.
- 🌍 **Multilingual Support:** Fully translated into 12 major languages.
- 🔒 **Privacy Focused & Local-First:** All your personal schedule data stays strictly on your device.
- 🛠️ **100% Open Source:** Fully transparent and open for community contributions.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Language:** [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
- **IDE:** [Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio)
- **Minimum SDK:** Android 8.0 (API level 26) or higher
- **UI Framework:** Jetpack Compose / Jetpack Components
- **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
